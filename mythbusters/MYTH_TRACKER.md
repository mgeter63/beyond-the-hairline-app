## Background footage rules (updated Nov 14)

- Animals are not required. Any calm, eye-catching footage works.
- Only licensed footage (Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons with a free license). Log the source for every clip.
- No real people without Mary's approval. That includes hands and faces.
- Rotate so no two videos in a row look alike.
- Ideas for upcoming videos: dandelion seeds blowing away, wheat or tall grass moving in the wind, silk fabric flowing, slow ocean waves, rain on a window, ink spreading in water, clouds in time-lapse, flowers opening in time-lapse.

## Wording rules (updated Nov 14)

- Speak to the viewer ("you"), not about a third person ("she").
- Never say or imply a scalp check or treatment brings hair back. Say early answers can stop or slow more loss.
- Scarring alopecia affects people of all races and ethnicities. Say plainly that CCCA is the type most tied to African American women.
- Blunt and direct, never scary or shaming. No em-dashes.
