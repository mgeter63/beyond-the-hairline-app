## Video 1 (Mon Nov 16): "It's just breakage."

Myth: "It's just breakage."
Fact: Breakage happens above the scalp. Alopecia begins below it.

Hair is replaceable. Hair follicles are not. When the follicle scars, that hair is gone. That's why a thinning crown deserves a closer look, not a trim and a shrug.

Barbers, cosmetologists, hairstylists, braiders, loctitians, and twist specialists: you see it first.

Scarring alopecia affects people of all races and ethnicities and is especially common among African American women. CCCA is the most common type in Black women.

## Video 2 (Wed Nov 18): "It'll grow back if you stop relaxing your hair."

Myth: "It'll grow back if you stop relaxing your hair."
Fact: Scarred follicles do not regrow from scar tissue.

Stopping or cutting back on relaxers does not bring back a follicle that has already scarred. Relaxers are not proven to be the only cause of CCCA. Early treatment can help stop or slow more loss, so get answers early.

Barbers, cosmetologists, hairstylists, braiders, loctitians, and twist specialists: when the crown changes, speak up.

## Video 3 (Fri Nov 20): "If it doesn't itch, it's not active."

Myth: "If it doesn't itch, it's not active."
Fact: CCCA can be silent. Many people have no burning, itching, or pain.

Silence does not equal safety. The first clue may simply be more scalp showing at the crown. Photos over time help show the change.

Barbers, cosmetologists, hairstylists, braiders, loctitians, and twist specialists: part through the crown and look.

## Video 4 (Mon Nov 23, noon Central, proposed): "Braids alone cause CCCA."

Myth: "Braids alone cause CCCA."
Fact: No single factor explains CCCA. Tight braids can make scalp inflammation worse.

CCCA can show up with braids, relaxers, wigs, natural hair, or no chemical history at all. Tension is still not harmless: years of tight styles can cause traction alopecia. If a style hurts, it's too tight, and scalp changes deserve a closer look.

Barbers, cosmetologists, hairstylists, braiders, loctitians, and twist specialists: you see the scalp up close. Speak up early.
