# How to Film Clips for Beyond the Hairline Text Reels

These clips are silent. Words, effects, and music get added after. You do not need to talk, but you can.

## Setup
- Phone held vertical (tall), at chest height. A tripod or a stack of books works.
- Face a window for soft light. Avoid a bright window behind you.
- Plain wall or tidy room behind you, a few feet away. A plain background makes the cutout cleaner.
- Wear a solid color that is different from the wall (your plum and gold look great).
- Frame yourself from the waist up, with space above your head. Big words go there.

## Clips to film (10 to 20 seconds each, a few takes of each)
1. Walk in from the side, stop in the middle, look at the camera.
2. Stand still, look at the camera, small nod, slight smile.
3. Point up to the space above your head, as if pointing at a sign.
4. Turn your head to the side, then back to the camera.
5. Walk toward the camera slowly.
6. Hands moving like you are explaining something (no need to speak).
7. Hold up a phone, a comb, or a jar like you are showing it.
8. Sit at a salon chair or table and look up at the camera.

## Tips
- Hold still for 2 seconds at the start and end of each clip.
- Move slowly. Fast motion blurs the cutout.
- Film in the highest quality your phone allows (4K if available).
- Send the original files, not screen recordings.

## Effects we can add
- Your video showing through big letters (title opener)
- A giant word behind you, with you standing in front of it
- Words that pop on screen one at a time
- End card: Recognize. Respond. Refer. / beyondthehairline.com
- Soft original music
