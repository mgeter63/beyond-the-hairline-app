Beyond the Hairline

Scarring Alopecia Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Your health information is personal. We take that seriously. This policy explains clearly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Important Notice: Beyond the Hairline is a personal self-tracking tool and is not a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). This app is not affiliated with any healthcare provider, insurance company, or medical institution. The data you enter is used for your personal self-management and for the longitudinal research study described in your informed consent form. Do not store information here that you would not be comfortable sharing outside of a clinical setting. For legally protected medical records, please use your healthcare provider's official patient portal.

1. Who We Are

Beyond the Hairline is operated by Chat Chew and Do Empowerment Women Group (CCDEW), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Jackson, Mississippi. The app is designed to help individuals living with scarring alopecia — including Central Centrifugal Cicatricial Alopecia (CCCA) and related conditions — track symptoms, document their journey, and prepare for medical appointments.

2. What Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Beyond the Hairline, we may collect the following:

  • Your email address (used for account creation and login)
  • Your phone number, if you choose to enable SMS verification
  • Health symptom logs you enter manually (such as pain levels, itching, shedding, and scalp changes)
  • Photos you choose to upload to document your hair and scalp over time
  • Doctor visit notes, questions, and appointment records you create
  • Treatment, product, and hair care information you choose to track
  • Hair practice history including products used, styling methods, and routines
  • Product purchase information including cost and frequency, if you choose to log it
  • Onset dates for symptoms and hair loss that you voluntarily enter
  • Gender identity you provide during profile setup
  • Racial and ethnic identity you provide during profile setup
  • Informed consent record including your typed name and date of consent
  • Hair services log including service type, cost, provider type, and date
  • Hair unit and hairpiece purchase log including item type, cost, and date
  • Hair products log including product category, brand name, cost, and date
  • Cosmetics and concealment log if applicable based on your identity and diagnosis
  • Your Care Journey responses including time to diagnosis, providers seen, cost barriers, and provider cultural competency
  • Scalp coverage percentage, symptom frequency, symptom trend, and symptom start date you report in each session

We do not collect your full legal name, home address, Social Security number, or payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to provide the features of this app to you. Specifically:

  • To display your personal health tracking data back to you
  • To allow you to document and review your health journey over time
  • To send account-related communications such as email confirmation and password reset
  • To send SMS verification codes if you have enabled that feature
  • To deliver identity-adaptive content, terminology, and product options tailored to your racial, ethnic, and gender identity
  • To send you daily affirming messages and wellness prompts through the app notification system as part of the Ecological Momentary Assessment research protocol

We do not sell your individually identifiable data. We do not share your data with advertisers. De-identified data may be used for research purposes and may be shared with or licensed to medical research organizations, pharmaceutical companies, or other research entities to advance scarring alopecia research, as described in your informed consent form.

4. Research Participation

Beyond the Hairline is currently conducting a longitudinal observational research study on scarring alopecia. Participants who enroll in the study provide informed consent before any data is collected for research purposes. Your de-identified data may be shared with or licensed to medical research organizations, pharmaceutical companies, or other research entities to advance scarring alopecia research and treatment development. You will not receive individual payment for this use. Any proceeds will be reinvested into scarring alopecia research and community education. You may withdraw from research participation at any time using the Withdraw from Study button in the app.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored securely using an industry-leading cloud database platform with industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS encryption in transit. Row-level security controls ensure that only you can access your personal records. Access to your account is protected by the credentials you create.

We strongly encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login credentials private. We will never ask for your password by email or phone.

6. SMS Communications

If you provide your phone number and enable SMS verification, your number will be used solely for account authentication purposes. We do not use your phone number for marketing. Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply. You may disable SMS at any time in your account settings.

7. Your Health Data Is Sensitive

We recognize that information about your hair loss condition, symptoms, photographs, and medical visits is sensitive personal health information. We treat it with the highest level of care. Only you can access your personal health records within this app. Our staff do not view your individual health entries.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data you have entered into the app at any time
  • Export your data in a readable format
  • Correct any inaccurate information in your account
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us
  • Opt out of any future research participation at any time
  • Withdraw consent for any use of your data beyond personal app functionality

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to request deletion. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. Anonymized, aggregated data that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for research and reporting purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Beyond the Hairline is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 21. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us immediately.

11. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — secure database and authentication
  • Vercel — app hosting and delivery

Each of these services has their own privacy policies and security standards. We have selected them based on their commitment to data security and privacy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify active users by email. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
